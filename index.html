🎵 SPOTIFY SETUP

The website uses one Client ID. Players only click Connect Spotify and sign in.

FOR PLAYERS

Just click Connect Spotify, sign into your normal Spotify account, and approve access. That is all.

ONE-TIME OWNER SETUP
  1. Go to the Spotify Developer Dashboard and create one app for this website.
  2. Copy the app's Client ID.
  3. Add this exact Redirect URI to that Spotify app:
  1. In Render, open your web service → Environment.
  2. Add SPOTIFY_CLIENT_ID as the key and paste your Client ID as the value.
  3. Save/redeploy the service, then refresh this website.
Do not put a Spotify Client Secret into the browser. This site uses the browser-safe PKCE login flow.
